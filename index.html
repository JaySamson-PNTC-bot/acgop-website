Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to bring ACGO to our school?

Our programs are affordable and flexible. Pricing depends on the type of program, number of students, and duration. We work with school budgets and offer options at different price points. Contact us for a customized quote.

How do we schedule programs around our school calendar?

We're flexible! Programs can be scheduled during school hours, after school, during assembly, or at other times that work for your school. We coordinate with your administration to find optimal timing.

Do your counselors have proper credentials?

Yes. Our team consists of certified career counselors and orientation specialists with proper education, training, and professional experience in career guidance.

What if we want to customize the program?

Customization is our strength! We work with each school to design programs that match your specific student population, goals, and needs.

Can we measure the results of the program?

Absolutely. We provide feedback, track student outcomes, and measure impact. You'll see student satisfaction ratings and evidence of program effectiveness.

What if we want ongoing support?

Perfect! We offer semester-long and annual partnership programs with ongoing support, counseling, and resources.
